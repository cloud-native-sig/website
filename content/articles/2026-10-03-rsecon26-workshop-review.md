Title: Kubechaos Returns to RSECon26
Date: 2026-10-03
Category: Events
Tags: events, kubernetes, workshop
Author: Fowler-Wright, Piper
Summary: Review of our Kubernetes deployment workshop at RSECon26

For this year's RSECon we brought back our popular Kubernetes deployment
workshop, now in its third iteration: `kubechaos:v3`.  If you haven't come
across it before, this tutorial walks through the deployment of a fun,
randomised web app called 'kubechaos', with participants running a local
Kubernetes cluster using minikube on their own laptops. We then looked at
Helm charts for pre-configured deployments, using the 'mocktail' application, which is
useful for testing HTTP requests, as an example.

The workshop ran on 10th September at the Wave in Sheffield
and was delivered by Alex, Piper and Lewis, with help from Dr Colin Sauze of the
National Oceanography Centre. Colin had contributed new troubleshooting steps
for installation on Linux systems to the lesson material, which were put to use
almost immediately!
Around 30 people joined us, with more attending online in our
first hybrid event. Thank you to everyone who took part and made for
a very engaging Q&A session.

Looking ahead, we have exciting plans to develop new lesson material, and
we'll soon have news on the SIG's ratification, so stay tuned. As always, our
[mailing list](https://www.jiscmail.ac.uk/cgi-bin/webadmin?A0=CLOUD-SIG) and
website remain the best way to keep up to date. If you're interested in
joining us, you can now reach any of us via our
[contact-us page](https://cloudnative-sig.ac.uk/pages/contact-us.html).

If you missed the workshop or want to revisit the material, the online
version is available at
[rsecon26-intro-to-kubernetes](https://cloud-native-sig.github.io/rsecon26-intro-to-kubernetes/).
