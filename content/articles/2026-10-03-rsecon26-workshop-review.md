Title: Kubechaos Returns to RSECon26
Date: 2026-10-03
Category: Events
Tags: events, kubernetes, workshop
Author: Fowler-Wright, Piper
Summary: Review of our Kubernetes deployment workshop at RSECon26

For this year's RSECon we brought back our popular Kubechaos workshop, now in
its third iteration.  If you haven't attended the workshop before, it takes you
through the deployment of a randomising web app, i.e., 'kubechaos', on a local
Kubernetes cluster using minikube. We also look at Helm charts for
pre-configured deployments, using the 'mocktail' application, which is useful
for testing HTTP requests, as an example.

The workshop ran on 10th September at the Wave in Sheffield and was delivered by
Alex, Piper and Lewis, with help from Dr Colin Sauze of the National
Oceanography Centre. Colin had added new troubleshooting steps for
installation on Linux systems to the lesson material, which found use almost
immediately! Around 30 people joined us, with more attending online in the
hybrid event. Thank you to everyone who took part and contributed to a very engaging
Q&A session.

Looking ahead, we have exciting plans to develop new lesson material, and
we'll soon have news on the SIG's ratification, so stay tuned. Our
[mailing list](https://www.jiscmail.ac.uk/cgi-bin/webadmin?A0=CLOUD-SIG) and
website remain the best way to keep up to date. If you're interested in
joining us, you can also now find our contact details on the
[contact-us page](https://cloudnative-sig.ac.uk/pages/contact-us.html) of the
website.

If you missed the workshop or want to revisit the material, the online
version is available at
[rsecon26-intro-to-kubernetes](https://cloud-native-sig.github.io/rsecon26-intro-to-kubernetes/).
