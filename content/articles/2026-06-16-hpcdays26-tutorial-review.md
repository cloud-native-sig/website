Title: Pocket-Sized Kubernetes at Durham HPC Days 2026
Date: 2026-06-18
Category: Events
Tags: events, kubernetes, workshop
Author: Fowler-Wright, Piper
Summary: A review of our hands-on Kubernetes cluster workshop at Durham HPC Days 2026

We were excited to deliver our brand new Kubernetes tutorial at Durham
HPC Days 2026. Titled *Pocket-Sized Kubernetes: Building and Deploying
with Raspberry Pi Clusters*, the tutorial was split into two parts.
The first saw participants build their own 4-node clusters using
Rancher's lightweight K3s distribution on Raspberry Pi hardware. The
second was exercise-based, allowing participants to learn Kubernetes
deployments and cluster management via a number of hands-on demos.

Some teething connectivity issues aside (yes, it is possible to
saturate a £20 TP-Link router!), the tutorial was a success, with
great engagement and feedback from attendees that we will carry
onwards&mdash;along with our newfound Pi hardware&mdash;to future
iterations of the tutorial. Thanks to everyone who took part and
offered interesting questions and suggestions.

All resources for the tutorial are available on
[GitHub](https://github.com/cloud-native-sig/hpcdays26-pocket-sized-kubernetes),
including instructions for following along at home with your own
hardware. We welcome anyone who wants to get involved with the SIG to
get in touch with [Lewis](mailto:lewis.sampson@stfc.ac.uk) or
[Piper](piper.fowler-wright@rfi.ac.uk), and sign up to our [mailing list](https://www.jiscmail.ac.uk/cgi-bin/wa-jisc.exe?SUBED1=CLOUDNATIVE-SIG).

Finally, thanks to CAKE for supporting our travel and delivery of the
workshop, and to the Durham HPC Days organisers for accommodating our
rather novel tutorial pitch!
