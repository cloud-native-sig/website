Title: Webinar: Showcasing Kubernetes for Research Infrastructure
Date: 2026-08-21
Category: Events
Tags: events, kubernetes, webinar
Author: Fowler-Wright, Piper
Summary: Summary of the SIG's first webinar, delivered for the ACIT Hub 

Yesterday, the Cloud-Native SIG presented its first-ever webinar,
*Kubernetes for Research Infrastructure - Core Concepts and Practical Use
Cases*, as part of the ongoing series hosted by the Accelerated Compute
Infrastructure Training Hub (ACIT Hub), which supports Research Infrastructure
Engineers (RIEs) across the UK.

Committee members Lewis, Tibor, Piper and Alex were joined by Christopher Green
from STFC SC to present a carousel of Kubernetes use cases in digital
research infrastructure, based on real-world deployments. The talks
covered:

* Kubernetes Overview
* Lab in the Cloud for IoN-DRI @ UCL
* A JupyterHub Training Platform
* Argo Workflows for Containerised Research Data Pipelines

It was great to see such a strong level of interest in the talks, with over 80
of the 92 registrants tuning in. 
Thank you to Dr Anna Swan from the University of Surrey for organising the
event and giving us the opportunity to share our applications of Kubernetes with
such a wide audience.

If you missed it, the recording is now available on
[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s6hw7WrLlqk), and the slides are
available on
[GitHub](https://github.com/cloud-native-sig/acithub26-k8s-webinar/tree/main).

We expect many of you will be interested in upcoming ACIT Hub events, including
a webinar and hackathon focused on bringing Slurm to Kubernetes. See the ACIT
Hub [Events list](https://acit.surrey.ac.uk/events.html) for further
information.

Our next Cloud-Native SIG appearance will be at RSECon26 on Thursday 11
September, and we hope to see many of you there.

In the meantime, if you’d like to get in touch or are interested in getting
involved with the SIG, head over to our [Contact Us
page](https://cloudnative-sig.ac.uk/pages/contact-us.html).
